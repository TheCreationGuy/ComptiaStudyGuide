TCP is connection oriented
    Sends responses that packages have been delivered
    "Reliable" delivery
    Has Flow control

UDP is connectionless with no response of errors
    No Flow control

Non-ephemeral ports are 0 through 1023
Ephemeral ports
    temporary

Ports Numbers are communication only

    -Generic way to transfer files
    -Fully Featured
        _List_Add_Delete_etc
    -Commonly associated with ports

SSH are text-based console used to manage files and send encrypted

Telnet- unencrypted way of communication
    Uses "In-the-clear" communication and has EVERYTHING visually shown unencrypted on user and host side

SMTP- easiest way to do server-to-server emails
    plaintext is in the clear and not used often
    SMTP is often used for email to phone services

    -v2 allows bulk data and enhanced data types using in-the-clear communication
    -v3 is a secure and modern standard.
        -Uses Authentication and Encryption
        -Encryption
        -Message Integrity
    SNMP trap sends alerts and notifications
        -great way for it to detect errors and communicate
